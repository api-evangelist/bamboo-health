# Bamboo Health

> Bamboo Health (formerly Appriss Health + PatientPing) is a healthcare technology company delivering Real-Time Care Intelligence across the physical and behavioral health continuum. It powers the nation's largest care-collaboration network — PDMP prescription monitoring, care coordination, and behavioral healthcare navigation — connecting health systems, health plans, pharmacies, and state agencies. No public developer API is published; integrations are delivered via EHR workflows and partner/state programs.
